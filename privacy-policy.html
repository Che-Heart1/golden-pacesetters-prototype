Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit the contact form on our website, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Your message or inquiry

2. How We Collect Your Data

We collect this information directly and only when you voluntarily submit the contact form on our website. No data is collected through cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or any form of passive monitoring.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not use your data for marketing, advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond directly answering your contact request. We will never sell or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Form submissions on this website are processed by Formspree, a third-party form handling service. When you submit the contact form, your data passes through Formspree's servers before being forwarded to us by email. Formspree operates under its own privacy policy, available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention

We do not store your personal data in any database, CRM, or file system. Your submission is delivered to us as an email notification and is not retained beyond that delivery. Golden Pacesetters does not archive form submissions on any server or platform under our control.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data you have submitted to us. To exercise this right, contact us at goldenpacesseters@gmail.com with a deletion request. We will confirm action within a reasonable timeframe. You also have the right to know what data we hold about you, and to correct any inaccuracies.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your personal data is handled, please reach out to us directly:
